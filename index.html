Research

  • VLPSA: safety filtering for learned policies

    A safety filter for vision-language-action policies that enforces full-body collision avoidance, including any grasped object, without retraining the policy. Dual-resolution Poisson safety functions composed as Boolean CBFs keep the filter real-time. On SafeLIBERO, collision avoidance for π0.5 rises from 23% to 91% with safe success rates preserved; deployed on hardware with dynamic obstacles.

    VLA policiessafety filteringSafeLIBERO

  • Grasp risk

    Flow matching is used to learn the uncertainty distributions inherent in RGB-D sensing that are relevant to grasp success. Novel uncertainty quantification and propagation yields bounds on risk metrics for grasp analysis. In progress.

    flow matchinggrasp analysisuncertainty quantification

  • Full-body dynamic safety for manipulators

    A 3D Poisson safety function is solved online from an occupancy map and used as a control barrier function over every link of the arm, with a novel sample-and-buffer method. The resulting CBF-QP filter runs at around 200 Hz on a Franka FR3 and handles fast dynamic obstacles. IROS 2026.

    control barrier functionsPoisson safety functionsFranka FR3

  • Optimal multi-target sensory coverage

    Shortest-path planning for a mobile robot that must observe a set of targets with a limited-range sensor, in open environments and among obstacles. The three problem variants are posed as MINLPs; polynomial-time approximations are given with proven bounds on the gap to the optimal path length.
